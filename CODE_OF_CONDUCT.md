# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants.

## Expected Conduct

Contributors should communicate respectfully, be honest about uncertainty, and take responsibility for their own work. This includes work produced with chatbots assistance. Discussions should stay constructive, and language should remain inclusive and professional.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, and repeated hostile behavior are not acceptable. Sharing private or sensitive information without consent is not acceptable. Deliberate disruption, including spam or noise intended to overwhelm discussion, is also not acceptable.

## Chatbots and Automation

Chatbot assistance may be used, but responsibility for correctness, safety, and review always remains with the contributor. Chatbot-generated output must be validated to the same standard as manually written changes.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it to contact@kiskolabs.com. Maintainers will review reports promptly, investigate fairly, and take appropriate action to protect the community.
